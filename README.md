# CASE: Creature Chaos

A silly, easy browser adventure for four people with human, flight, and chaos forms. Start running through a forest, then find out why everyone ends up floating in space.

## Current forms

| Character | Human ability | Flight form | Chaos form |
|---|---|---|---|
| C | Improvised vault | Flamingo | Red fox |
| A | Protective force | Raven (peacock for 5 seconds with R) | Giant black wolf |
| S | Rapid plan | Crow | Slim black panther |
| E | Quick thinking | Snowy owl (great horned owl with R for five seconds) | Cheetah |

The game opens in human form in a playable forest level. Run to the glowing portal to continue into space, then enter the backyard. C’s flamingo takes visible high steps on the ground. E’s default flight form is a snowy owl; R briefly shifts E into the great horned owl. S can turn into a bat for five seconds with R while in flight form. Hold W while in a flight form to climb, with a screen-top limit.

Aquatic forms and additional animal groups are intentionally reserved for much later unlock systems. Mythical forms are being explored: C’s is a kraken, A’s is a giant black land dragon, and S and E are undecided.

## Controls

- Move: `A` / `D` or left/right arrows
- Jump: `W`, `Space`, or up arrow
- Crouch / drop: `S` or down arrow
- Ability: `E`
- Special: `R` (A becomes a peacock, S a bat, and E a great horned owl for five seconds in flight form; A's wolf uses pack-force smash in chaos form)
- Wolf bite: `E` while A is in chaos form
- Transform: `F` or `Shift` cycles Human → Flight → Chaos
- Switch character: `1`–`4` or the on-screen character dock

Touch controls are included for phones and tablets.
